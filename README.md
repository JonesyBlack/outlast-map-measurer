# outlast-map-measurer
Use of this tool requires you to launch the tool and press z once you are looking at the left side of a wall, with your dot exactly on it. Then, you will aim your dot to the bottom left corner of the wall and then press z again once you are on it. The amount of time it takes you to do this does not matter, as well if you accidentally overshoot or undershoot and then correct while you haven't pressed z yet.

If you, at any point, need to reset the process, press r. You cannot undo, as that would result in a lot of human error, you wouldn't be able to get back to the exact same point, and the script would have to assume you did.

Once you've pressed z on the left corner of the wall, you will move to look at the bottom right corner of the wall and then press z.

After you have pressed z, you will move your mouse up until you are at "eye" level once again, and then press z again. 

At this point, a dialogue box will open telling you how many outlasttrialsstats.com map editor gridspaces the wall is long, and/or will allow you to copy a snippet of json code that is formatted in the same way as the json files used internally for outlasttrialsstats.com's map editor.

once you close the dialogue box, the tool will restart, allowing you to measure a new wall without re-opening. If you want to close the app, press right-alt.
